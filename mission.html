Our mission

Hello, we are Hope Link Foundation, where together we raise hope!! We are a student-led organization that raises money to support people experiencing homelessness.

According to UnitedNations.org, there are 150 million homeless people around the world, and almost 1 million in the USA alone! To solve this situation we have created a foundation called the Hope Link Foundation where we raise money through our GoFundMe fundraising and donate 100% of all donations to charities such as the Sunday Breakfast Mission, where they provide shelter, materials, and health care for the homeless. With one single donation you can change many lives!

As of now we have teamed up with organizations such as Main Street’s GreenHouse, where we placed a donation box at their place. We did this so that people could donate in public areas.
